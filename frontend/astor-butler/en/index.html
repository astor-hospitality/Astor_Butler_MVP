For restaurants

Ready requests arrive in the team’s work chat

Astor works inside the tools your team already uses: Telegram and Saby. The hostess needs no new screen, the work group is enough.

  • Requests in the staff group

    Bookings and lunch orders arrive as cards: time, table, guests, lunch items. The hostess answers with “Yes” or “No”. Requests, feedback and the shift schedule live in their own topics in the same group.

  • Menu and order in Saby

    The lunch menu and prices come from the Saby price list, and a booking with dishes is written to Presto. The restaurant’s till calculates the bill, not the bot.

  • Manager board

    Today’s bookings and lunches for the shift manager, visits by stage from request to review, and time to confirmation. The board only shows; decisions stay with the team.

  • Guest reviews

    After the visit the guest gives a score from 1 to 5. Scores go to the team and are collected on the manager board.

  • Guests bring friends

    The app has an “Invite friends” button: the guest sends a personal link, and new guests arrive tagged with their source.

  • Your restaurant in the Astor app

    Guests find you in the Astor Mini App: curated places, table requests, business lunch and questions to the restaurant in one Telegram window.

Astor rules

AI runs the conversation, the restaurant keeps the commitments

  • The restaurant confirms the booking

    AI collects the request; the hostess or the till sets the “confirmed” status.

  • The till names the amount

    The bill and payment come from Saby. The bot never makes up numbers.

  • One guest consent

    One consent covers the concierge and the restaurant bot. Phone numbers are hidden on the board.

  • Data stays in Russia

    Servers on Cloud.ru, language models by GigaChat.

For partners

Yekaterinburg – Doha: we gather the demand first

  • A waitlist, not a sale

    Guests join the waitlist for a direct Yekaterinburg – Doha trip in the Astor app: month, party size, format. Partners see demand by month and source.

  • Deposits go to the tour operator

    A refundable deposit is paid only to a registered tour operator. Astor never holds guest money.

  • Your logo once payment is confirmed

    Until payment is confirmed, a partner appears in grey, without a logo, marked “in negotiation”. Ads are labelled as required by Russian law.

  • Astor Visit

    Country programmes in the “Where to go” and “Events” sections, hosted by the national tourism partner. Astor alone adds and curates the events.
